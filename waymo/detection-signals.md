# Waymo track detection signals: internal engineering onboarding

No DNS or page-level signals exist for internal programs, so every signal here is a people or growth signal.

| # | Signal | How to detect | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engineering headcount growth above ~25% YoY | Revelio-style trackers, LinkedIn insights | High |
| 2 | New international engineering hubs | Press, job postings by location | High |
| 3 | Enablement hire from Uber, Twitter, Google, Meta class programs, within 90 days | LinkedIn saved search on role changes | Highest, this is timing |
| 4 | L&D sits under People with Specialist or PM titles, no engineering-side education org | LinkedIn department and title mapping | Medium, predicts the sponsorship fight |
| 5 | Multiple disconnected training functions visible | LinkedIn, as above | Medium |
| 6 | No LMS admin roles posted despite training growth | Job boards | Medium, implies Drive-based content |
| 7 | Learning community participation (TechKnowCon and similar) | Attendee and speaker lists | Warm-lead source |

## How the signals compose

* Signals 1 and 2 define the candidate pool: fast-growing engineering orgs going multi-hub, where in-person onboarding mathematically stops scaling.
* Signal 3 is the trigger. A champion who ran a mature enablement program at a big-tech company feels the gap at the new company on day one, and their first 90 days are the rethink window.
* Signals 4 and 5 predict the internal fight: a Specialist under HR owning an engineering-wide program has the problem but not the sponsor. Outreach and events strategy should arm the champion, not just pitch them.
* Signal 6 corroborates the stack: training growth with no LMS admin hiring means content lives in docs and drives.
* Signal 7 is the warm path. Learning community attendee and speaker lists are a reverse-engineerable warm-lead source: everyone there is the ICP for this motion.

## Contrast with the main track

| | Main track (customer education) | Waymo track (internal onboarding) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary evidence | Infrastructure: DNS, URL paths, rendering, llms.txt, Wayback | People: LinkedIn roles, departments, role changes, job boards |
| Timing signal | Platform age in the 3.5 to 5.5 year renewal window | Champion's first 90 days in seat |
| Confirmation | The academy itself | Org chart shape plus hiring exhaust |
| Shared rule | Ex-employees and personal LinkedIn posts leak the most | Same |
