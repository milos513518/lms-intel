# Waymo track: the internal engineering onboarding signature

Same method as the main track, different playbook. The anchor here is **internal engineering enablement** (onboarding engineers at a fast-scaling company), not customer education. That flips the detection model: no DNS, no academy pages, no Wayback dates. **This playbook is people-first where the customer education playbook is infrastructure-first.** The two share one rule: ex-employees and personal LinkedIn posts leak the most.

## The anchor pattern (all externally verifiable)

* Engineering headcount growing ~40% YoY, roughly 1,500 engineers of 2,400 to 3,000 total
* New international engineering hubs (Warsaw as the only EU engineering center and fastest growing location, Hyderabad, Oxford via acquisition, Tokyo), which breaks any fly-to-HQ onboarding model
* At least five separate training functions visible on LinkedIn, with no LMS admin roles posted anywhere, which corroborates fragmented, Drive-based content without any inside information
* The program owner sits under People with a Specialist title, owning an engineering-wide program, so the sponsorship gap is visible in the org chart itself
* The owner is a recent hire from a big-tech enablement program (Uber diaspora), inside her first 90 days

## The repeatable signature

See `detection-signals.md` for the full table. The headline: **the single best timing signal in this playbook is a champion who recently joined from a big-tech enablement team (Uber, Twitter, Google, Meta class programs), within their first 90 days.** That is literally a LinkedIn saved search: new role + enablement background + People department + scaling engineering company. It makes this playbook as repeatable as the DNS sweep on the main track.
